Frequently Asked Questions

TripKIRA driver help and common questions.

How do I register for TripKIRA?

Open TripKIRA and tap REGISTRATION. Complete the Google Form. Your Membership ID is issued separately after registration is processed.

Why do I need Device Activation?

Device Activation links the Membership ID to an approved device and registered email before protected TripKIRA functions can be used.

When should I tap START?

Tap START when the passenger trip begins. TripKIRA records the start time and Point A information.

When should I tap END?

Tap END when the passenger trip is completed, then enter the fare and save the trip.

When should I use SHARE?

Use SHARE to upload/share saved trip records. If the daily ending odometer is missing, TripKIRA may ask for it before sharing.

Why does Daily Check-In not appear when I open TripKIRA?

The launch check-in screen only appears when today's check-in has not already been claimed. It remains available in Inbox.

Where can I change language, theme and voice?

From v3.32, tap SETTINGS. Theme, app language and voice settings are grouped there.

Where can I download the latest TripKIRA?

Use HELP → Download or visit the TripKIRA Download page on this website.
